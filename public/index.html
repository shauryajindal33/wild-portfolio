How this prototype works

Map — drag anywhere to walk. Reaching a plant or animal opens a prompt asking whether to harvest/capture it or leave it — nothing happens automatically. The map is large; rarer animals are genuinely hard to find.

Captivity — "Wild population" shows every species' numbers in the wild. "Your portfolio" is where captured animals live: feed, convert, release, or send a trusted one to the zoo.

Feeding is gated to 50% hunger or below. Trust starts to decline at 25% hunger or below.

Legacy value is rarity plus how costly the animal is to acquire and raise. It only counts once an animal is secured in the zoo — a held animal shows its potential value, not yet added to your total.

Zoo — 3 free cages, unlocking to 5 at 300 secured Legacy and 7 at 500. Only one individual of each species can be in the zoo at a time.

Releasing from the zoo is allowed — it frees the cage and drops that Legacy from your total, but immediately doubles that species' trust requirement. The next one you secure will need far more trust, but will be worth proportionally more Legacy. Releasing from your portfolio (before zoo) instead raises trust by 25%, compounding per release.

Convert turns a trusted herbivore/omnivore into meat and permanently lowers its wild population.

Terrain is illustrative artwork, not a real map. Timings are compressed and approximate for testing.
